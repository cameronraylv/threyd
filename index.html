Questions

FAQ

What does "managed" actually mean?

It means we don’t stop at placement. From recruiting and onboarding to coaching, QA, and daily escalations, your team's performance is our responsibility.

How do you measure performance?

We establish KPIs at the start of every engagement — tailored to your operation, not borrowed from a generic template. From there, you receive weekly reporting with full visibility into how your team is performing. If something needs adjusting, we identify it and address it.

What happens if it isn't working?

We stand behind every placement with a 90-day replacement guarantee. If the fit isn't right, we rebuild at no additional cost. That said, we stay involved throughout the engagement precisely to catch problems early — most issues are resolved long before they become decisions.

How quickly can we get started?

Most engagements go from intake to launch in two to three weeks. We move fast, but not at the expense of fit. The goal isn’t to fill a seat, it’s to build a team worth keeping.

What does this cost?

Pricing is scoped to the role, the skills required, and the hours needed. You’ll get one clear monthly rate that covers both the talent and Threyd’s ongoing management — no separate recruiting fees, no surprise add-ons.

Who are the professionals on your team, and where are they based?

Our professionals are based internationally, recruited specifically for your role rather than assigned from a standing pool. We pay above-market rates for the regions we hire in — not only because it's the ethical thing to do, but because it produces the quality and retention our clients rely on. Engaged, fairly compensated professionals deliver better support, and your customers feel the difference.
